This folder represents the **six core policies** delivered as part of the CTFCU GRC Program — the foundational policy set required by ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clauses 5.2, 6.1, 7.5, 8.1, 9.1, and 10.

| # | Policy | Purpose | ISO 27001 Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Information Security Policy** | The "master" policy — establishes management commitment, scope, and information security objectives | Clause 5.2 |
| 2 | **Access Control Policy** | Governs identity, authentication, authorization, and privileged access management | Annex A.5.15–A.5.18 |
| 3 | **Acceptable Use Policy** | Defines acceptable use of CTFCU information systems by employees and contractors | Annex A.5.10, A.6.3 |
| 4 | **Third-Party Risk Management Policy** | Governs vendor onboarding, due diligence, ongoing monitoring, and offboarding | Annex A.5.19–A.5.22 |
| 5 | **Incident Response Policy** | Defines incident detection, classification, escalation, response, and post-incident review | Annex A.5.24–A.5.28 |
| 6 | **Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery Policy** | Governs continuity of operations, recovery time and recovery point objectives | Annex A.5.29–A.5.30 |

## Included Sample

The full text of **Policy #1 — Information Security Policy** is included in this folder as a representative example: [`01-Information-Security-Policy.md`](./01-Information-Security-Policy.md).

This is the foundational policy that ISO auditors look for first during a Stage 1 certification audit. It is signed by the CEO, reviewed annually by the Board's Risk Committee, and serves as the umbrella under which all other policies, standards, and procedures operate.

The remaining five policies follow the same template, scope, and review cadence — they are referenced here rather than published in full to keep this portfolio repository focused and readable.
